What a licensed mindset coach changes

You can see everyone's blind spots except your own. That is not a flaw. It is how paradigms work, and it is exactly what a coach is for.

A licensed coach walks you through the 12 modules with someone watching the process who has done it themselves: keeping you honest in the daily practice, catching the old pattern when it dresses up as a good excuse, and holding your goal steady when your comfort zone negotiates for something smaller.

Guided clients move faster for one reason: the moment the work gets uncomfortable is the moment most people quit alone, and the coach is there for precisely that moment.
