The self study mindset program: start today, on your terms

The self study mindset program is the entry door to The Inner Blueprint: all 12 modules, a daily practice structure, an active community, and always-on support, moving at the pace you set. One program fee, plus an ongoing membership for community and support.

SEC 01 / CONTENTS

What It Is

The 12 modules

The full curriculum in short, focused lessons designed for daily listening. The same material coached clients work through, structured so repetition does the heavy lifting.

The daily ritual

A guided routine of minutes a day: lesson, goal focus, affirmations, gratitude. Small on the calendar, decisive over months.

The community

Ongoing membership puts you among people doing the same work, sharing wins and holding standards. Change sticks better in company.

AI Shawn

An always-on assistant trained on Shawn's methodology. Stuck on a lesson at 6 a.m. before the crew arrives? Ask, and keep moving.

GRID A / METHOD
SEC 02 / METHOD

How It Works

One module per week, listened to daily. Two full passes through the curriculum. That is the initial arc, and it is designed that way on purpose.

The first pass introduces the ideas. The second pass installs them, because by then you are hearing the material with different ears. Members consistently report that round two is where the deep rewiring happens.

You are never rushed and never alone: the community and AI Shawn travel with you the whole way.

SEC 04 / QUESTIONS

FAQ

How much time does self study take each day?

Plan for about half an hour: a short lesson plus the daily ritual of goal focus, affirmations, and gratitude. The commitment is small by design. Consistency is what does the work.

What does it cost?

There is a one time program fee plus an ongoing membership for community access and AI Shawn. Reach out through the contact page and we will walk you through current options and what fits your situation.

Can I move to a mastermind or a coach later?

Yes, and many members do. Self study gives you the foundation, and the step up to mastermind groups or one on one coaching is seamless whenever you are ready.
