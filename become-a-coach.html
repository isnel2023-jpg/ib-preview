Who It's For

This license is for people who have lived the work: leaders, builders, and mentors whose own transformation makes others ask what changed.

Some of our strongest coaches come from business ownership, sales leadership, and the trades, because they coach from experience rather than theory. If people already come to you for direction, this is the structure that turns that gift into a practice.

It is not for collectors of certifications. The license comes with a standard, and the standard is upheld.

The Trifecta

Build your own version of a 10

This work is not about copying anyone else's life. It is about building a 10 in each area of your own Trifecta, and that looks different for every coach.

01

Finances

A practice that pays you for the depth of your work, not for the hours you manage to sell. What a 10 means here is yours to define: security, freedom, or the room to be generous.

02

Health and Wellness

The energy to hold space for other people without emptying your own. A body and a mind you have rebuilt on purpose, because you cannot coach a standard you do not live.

03

Relationships

The people closest to you feeling the change before your clients do. You coach from a full life, not from a performance of one.

Whatever your 10 looks like, it is yours. Not ours. The license gives you the method and the standard; what you build with it is the point.

One method. One standard.

What a licensed coach receives

01

The methodology

Full training in the 12 module curriculum and the coaching framework around it. You deliver a system with years of results behind it, protected by trademark and license. Licensed coaches complete their certification during the first year, while they are already coaching.

02

The materials

Client curriculum access, brand assets, and coach resources, ready to use. You focus on coaching. The infrastructure is already built.

03

The platform

A profile in the official coach directory, co-branded presence, and a brand you can promote as your own practice grows with it.

04

The community

You join the coaches who are building alongside you, with direct access to the creators and the collective experience of the network.

How coaches earn

Three streams, one practice

A license is not a job. It is a business with more than one way to grow, and the streams compound as your practice does.

  1. 01

    One to one coaching

    The foundation. Clients working through the program with you, at the depth only a private room allows.

  2. 02

    Group masterminds

    Leveraged income. One room, several clients, one standard, and a community that keeps them coming back.

  3. 03

    Team building override

    Recurring revenue. Coaches you bring into the network generate an initiation fee plus a recurring monthly share for you. You build a team, not just a client list.

Is this you?

This license is built for a specific kind of person. Read honestly.

This is for you if

  • You've done real personal transformation work yourself, and it changed you
  • People already come to you for guidance, and you're ready to make it a practice
  • You're an existing coach, therapist, or mentor looking for a proven system instead of building your own from scratch
  • You lead people well, at work, in your community, in your family, and you've been told you should be coaching
  • You want to build something of your own, not just add a certification to your resume
  • You're willing to invest in your own growth before asking anyone else to invest in theirs
  • You want a business model with recurring revenue and a way to build a team, not just trade hours for dollars

This is not for you if

  • You're looking for a quick certification to add to your LinkedIn
  • You want a done-for-you business with no work required
  • You haven't done the inner work yourself and aren't willing to
  • You're not open to feedback, coaching, or being held to a standard
  • You want overnight results or income guarantees
  • You're only interested in the money and not the transformation
